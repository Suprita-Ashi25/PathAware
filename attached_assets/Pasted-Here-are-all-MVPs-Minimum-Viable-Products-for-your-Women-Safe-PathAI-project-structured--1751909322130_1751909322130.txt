# 🚀 Women Safe PathAI – MVP Roadmap

> 🌟 **Goal**: Deliver safety to women using AI with **zero manual interaction**
> 📍 Focused for **India**, built with **privacy, autonomy, and local usability**

---

## ✅ **MVP 1 – Panic Detection + Auto SOS** (Core Launch Version)

### 🎯 Goal:

Detect danger automatically using phone sensors and audio, and send SOS without buttons.

### 🔑 Features:

* 🔁 Background running on phone
* 📳 **Shake detection**, **fall detection**, **sudden running**
* 🔊 **Scream detection** using mic + AI (YAMNet or Whisper)
* 📍 GPS-based location tracking
* 🚨 Auto-SOS: sends SMS + location to trusted contacts
* 🛑 Offline fallback (SMS alert if no internet)

### 🔧 Stack:

* React Native + Expo Sensors
* Flask (Python) + YAMNet
* Firebase Realtime DB or Twilio for alerts

---

## 🔄 **MVP 2 – Voice AI & Local Language Assistant**

### 🎯 Goal:

Create an always-listening voice assistant that can detect tone and whisper commands like “Help me”.

### 🔑 Features:

* 🎙️ **Whisper-to-Text AI**
* 🧠 Voice tone/emotion detection (fear, panic)
* 🔑 Wake words like “Save me” or “Help PathAI” (even whispered)
* 🗣️ Assistant replies in **Hindi, Telugu, English**
* 🧏 Text-to-Speech (Coqui / gTTS / Google TTS)
* 🔐 Anonymous voice chat with AI — no tapping needed

### 🔧 Stack:

* Whisper + EmotionNet
* Google TTS / gTTS
* Multilingual support (i18n)

---

## 🔄 **MVP 3 – Safe Path AI + Crime Risk Detection**

### 🎯 Goal:

Automatically suggest the safest walking route based on open crime data and CCTV zones.

### 🔑 Features:

* 📍 Real-time safe route suggestion (maps + risk overlay)
* 🔥 Crime heatmap based on NCRB or local police data
* 🚧 Road quality, street lighting, and CCTV awareness
* 🧠 ML model to rate routes from 0 (danger) to 5 (safe)
* 🧭 If user enters danger zone, app switches to Companion Mode

### 🔧 Stack:

* OpenStreetMap + Google Maps
* Custom route AI model
* Crime data: NCRB, public portals

---

## 🔄 **MVP 4 – Community Trust & Safety Map**

### 🎯 Goal:

Enable women to anonymously report and mark safe/unsafe places — building a crowd-sourced trust map.

### 🔑 Features:

* 📍 “Mark as Safe/Unsafe” zones (cafés, alleys, autos)
* 👩‍👧 Reports from women stored anonymously
* 🔁 Backend moderation and auto-trust scoring
* 🗺️ Trusted local places shown (verified by women/NGOs)
* 📚 Public view of daily incidents (no personal data)

### 🔧 Stack:

* Firebase DB + Node.js APIs
* Geo-tagging and scoring system
* Optional: Police verification API

---

## 🔄 **MVP 5 – NGO, Police, and Real Human Mesh**

### 🎯 Goal:

Connect users to **verified real-world help** – NGOs, police booths, female-safe shelters, local guides.

### 🔑 Features:

* 👮 Police location + contact API (India112 + local)
* 🤝 NGO partners can offer digital help line
* 🆘 Emergency call routing if panic mode triggered
* 👩 Human guardians – opt-in volunteer system
* 📡 Shared dashboard for verified help centers

### 🔧 Stack:

* Integration with 112/100/WhatsApp APIs
* NGO verification dashboard
* Voice bot to forward requests

---

## 🔄 **MVP 6 – Offline Mode + Edge AI**

### 🎯 Goal:

Even without network, PathAI should function: detect, predict, and react to threats.

### 🔑 Features:

* 🔌 Edge AI for emotion & scream detection
* 📍 Preloaded safe zones & trusted centers
* 🧠 Offline AI fallback for panic mode
* 🔒 SOS triggers via SMS, Bluetooth, sound pulse

### 🔧 Stack:

* TensorFlow Lite (on-device)
* SQLite / AsyncStorage
* Offline TTS/STT models

---

## 🔄 **MVP 7 – Trust Circle & Companion AI**

### 🎯 Goal:

Allow users to form a **circle of guardians** who are always alerted in Companion Mode.

### 🔑 Features:

* 👭 Invite trusted contacts
* 🔔 Live route sharing with AI commentary
* 🛑 No movement or off-route → auto-alert to all
* 🗣️ AI companion talks: "I’m watching, you’re safe."
* 🎧 3D spatial sound & deterrent noise in threat zones

---

## 🔄 **MVP 8 – Real-time Multi-Language AI Interface**

### 🎯 Goal:

Complete natural language interaction for women across India — no touch, only talk.

### 🔑 Features:

* 🧏 Voice-only interaction (Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil…)
* 📥 Ask: “Where is the nearest safe café?”
* 🧠 AI replies with spoken directions
* 🎤 Handles noisy environments and partial speech
* 👩‍💼 Elder-friendly + blind-friendly design
